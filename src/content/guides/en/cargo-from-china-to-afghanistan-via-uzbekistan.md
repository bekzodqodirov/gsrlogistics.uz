GSR Logistics is a logistics company in Tashkent, Uzbekistan; since 2018 it has carried cargo from China to Uzbekistan. It now also carries cargo to Afghanistan by truck. In China, cargo for Afghanistan is received in Yiwu; from there it travels by truck through Kazakhstan and Uzbekistan. The cargo crosses Uzbekistan in transit and leaves through the Termez–Hairatan crossing; you collect it at Hairatan, or we deliver it to Mazar-i-Sharif. Below: the journey stage by stage, the border crossing, the documents, price and timing, and how to start.

## How does cargo from China get to Afghanistan through Uzbekistan?

GSR Logistics carries cargo from China to Afghanistan by truck: the cargo is received in Yiwu, goes through Kazakhstan into Uzbekistan, crosses Uzbekistan under customs transit and leaves through the Termez–Hairatan crossing. On the Afghan side, customs clearance is done by our partners — customs agents (declarants). You collect the cargo at Hairatan, or we deliver it to Mazar-i-Sharif.

The journey in five stages:

| Stage | What happens | Who does it |
|---|---|---|
| Receiving in China | The cargo is received in Yiwu | We receive the cargo in Yiwu |
| The road to Uzbekistan | The cargo travels by truck through Kazakhstan | We organise the carriage |
| Across Uzbekistan | Customs transit: a transit declaration (Customs Code, Article 118), customs seals or other means of identification (Article 221) and a delivery deadline set by the customs office of departure (Articles 120 and 218) | Our own customs broker files the transit declaration |
| Termez–Hairatan | The cargo leaves Uzbekistan and goes through customs on the Afghan side | On the Afghan side, customs clearance is done by our partners — customs agents (declarants) |
| Handover | The cargo is handed over at Hairatan or in Mazar-i-Sharif | At Hairatan you collect it yourself; to Mazar-i-Sharif we deliver it to the city |

The transit regime ends when the goods are taken out of the customs territory (Customs Code, Article 120, part one). The procedure inside Uzbekistan is described in detail in the [Transit through Uzbekistan: documents and procedure](/en/guides/transit-through-uzbekistan-documents/) guide.

## Where does the truck cross into Afghanistan?

Uzbekistan and Afghanistan have one border crossing, Termez–Hairatan, according to the website of the President of Uzbekistan (president.uz, June 1, 2021). On the Uzbek side the crossing is the Ayritom border customs post, in Termez district (Surxondaryo region). The crossing is a bridge over the Amu Darya, usually called the Friendship Bridge (the Termez–Hairatan bridge).

Trucks and trains both cross here. We carry cargo to Afghanistan by truck only.

Hairatan is the Afghan border town on the far side of the bridge. Mazar-i-Sharif is about 80 km from Hairatan by road.

## Where is the cargo handed over: Hairatan or Mazar-i-Sharif?

We hand over cargo for Afghanistan at Hairatan or in Mazar-i-Sharif. Today these are the two delivery points:

- **Hairatan** — the border town on the Afghan side: you collect the cargo there yourself.
- **Mazar-i-Sharif** — we deliver the cargo to the city.

Other cities in Afghanistan are not among today’s delivery points.

## Is customs duty paid in Uzbekistan on cargo that only passes through?

Under the transit regime the goods cross Uzbekistan without payment of customs duties and taxes, such as import duty, value added tax and excise tax (Customs Code, Articles 116 and 289); customs fees are still charged (Cabinet of Ministers Resolution No. 55 of January 31, 2025), and if the goods are lost or handed over to other persons without the consent of customs, the carrier must pay the customs payments (Customs Code, Article 219, part three).

In return the goods travel under a transit declaration (Customs Code, Article 118), under customs seals or other means of identification (Article 221), within a delivery deadline set by the customs office of departure (Articles 120 and 218), and with no cargo operations without the consent of customs (Article 219); reloading and temporary storage on the way are possible only under the control of the intermediate customs office (Customs Code, Article 119).

The customs fee for a transit declaration and the law’s time limits are in the [Transit through Uzbekistan: documents and procedure](/en/guides/transit-through-uzbekistan-documents/) guide. Duties on goods imported into Uzbekistan are a different subject, covered in the [Uzbekistan customs duties 2026](/en/guides/uzbekistan-customs-duties-2026/) guide.

On the Afghan side, customs clearance is done by our partners — customs agents (declarants). The manager explains which procedure and which documents apply on the Afghan side for your cargo.

## Which documents are needed, and who handles them?

You provide the commercial invoice and the packing list from your supplier, and we tell you which other documents we need from you; we handle the transit documents inside Uzbekistan. Each document, who has it and what we do:

| Document | Who has it | What we do |
|---|---|---|
| Commercial invoice and packing list | You, from your supplier | The transit declaration is filed from them |
| CMR consignment note | Made out in three originals signed by the sender and the carrier: the first for the sender, the second travels with the goods, the third stays with the carrier (CMR Convention, Article 5, paragraph 1) | We prepare and check it |
| TIR Carnet, where the carriage is under a TIR Carnet | The carrier: it is issued to the carrier by the authorised national association of the carrier’s own country; where advance information has been submitted to customs, a TIR Carnet is accepted as the transit declaration (Cabinet of Ministers Resolution No. 737, point 2) | We check it |
| Transit declaration in Uzbekistan | Filed with customs electronically, through the Customs Committee’s E-Tranzit system (Customs Code, Article 257-1, part two; Cabinet of Ministers Resolution No. 737 of December 31, 2022, Regulation point 8) | Our own customs broker files it |
| Security for customs payments | One of the three ways customs secures a truck carrying foreign goods; without it, and outside the other exceptions (for example a TIR Carnet), the truck is escorted (Customs Code, Articles 222 and 223) | We help arrange it |
| Certificates and permits for the goods, where needed | A certificate of conformity, a phytosanitary certificate and other documents | We help obtain them |
| Afghan customs | On the Afghan side, customs clearance is done by our partners — customs agents (declarants) | The manager explains which procedure and which documents apply on the Afghan side for your cargo |

The CMR Convention applies to a contract for the carriage of goods by road for reward when the place of taking over of the goods and the place designated for delivery, as specified in the contract, are in two different countries, at least one of which is a contracting country (CMR Convention, Article 1, paragraph 1). So a load from China to Uzbekistan or Afghanistan can fall under it. Uzbekistan and Kazakhstan are parties to the CMR Convention, and China is not. Afghanistan acceded to the CMR Convention in October 2020; it has applied to Afghanistan since January 5, 2021.

China, Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan and Afghanistan are all contracting parties to the TIR Convention. This is the status of the conventions; it does not mean that a particular truck travels under a TIR Carnet.

Our part of this paperwork is described on the page [Transit documents in Uzbekistan](/en/services/transit-documents/); the CMR and other cargo terms are explained in the [Cargo glossary](/en/guides/cargo-glossary/).

## A full truck or consolidated cargo: which one fits your load?

We carry both: a full truck when your cargo takes the whole truck, and consolidated cargo when it does not — several clients’ cargo then travels in one truck, and small lots are accepted.

| Option | What it is | When it fits |
|---|---|---|
| A full truck | The whole truck is given to one client’s cargo | Your cargo fills a truck, or you want the truck for your cargo alone |
| Consolidated cargo | Several clients’ cargo travels in one truck; small lots are accepted | Your lot is too small for a whole truck |

The smallest lot is agreed with the manager; this guide gives no figure for it.

## How much does it cost, and how long does it take?

Price and timing are on request: the manager calculates them for your cargo. The calculation depends on what the cargo is, its volume and weight, whether it travels as a full truck or as consolidated cargo, whether it goes to Hairatan or to Mazar-i-Sharif, and which documents and permits it needs.

The tariffs, the calculator and the transit times on this site apply to the China → Tashkent direction only; the 1 m³ minimum published on this site applies to consolidated cargo from China to Tashkent. None of these applies to cargo for Afghanistan. The [China–Tashkent routes](/en/guides/china-tashkent-routes/) guide, too, describes how cargo travels from China to Tashkent, not to Afghanistan.

## Can the cargo change trucks or wait in Tashkent?

Yes: in Tashkent we reload cargo truck to truck or through a partner’s warehouse. We allocate space for our clients in that warehouse, where cargo is stored under customs control; the warehouse belongs to the partner, not to GSR. Details are on the page [Warehouse under customs control in Tashkent](/en/services/customs-warehouse-tashkent/).

Under Article 119, part one, of the Customs Code, goods in transit may be transshipped, under the control of the intermediate customs office, from the vehicle that brought them into Uzbekistan to the vehicle that will take them out. Under the same control they may be unloaded and temporarily warehoused — stored, split into consignments or built up into consignments and similar operations — in places of temporary storage (Customs Code, Article 119, part one); temporary storage under Article 119 of the Customs Code is used to suspend the running of the transit term, including where the vehicle is damaged.

## Can you also carry cargo from Uzbekistan to Afghanistan?

Yes: we also carry cargo that is already in Uzbekistan to Afghanistan by truck, as export, to the same delivery points — Hairatan or Mazar-i-Sharif.

Under point 7 of the Regulation approved by Cabinet of Ministers Resolution No. 737 of December 31, 2022, a transit declaration is not required, among other cases, for goods and vehicles leaving the country (except goods under the re-export regime).

For such cargo the manager names the documents you need and tells you where we take the cargo over.

## What does GSR Logistics do on this route, and how do you start?

GSR Logistics carries cargo to Afghanistan by truck and handles the transit documents inside Uzbekistan. On this route we offer:

- **Carriage by truck.** We carry cargo from China, where it is received in Yiwu, and from Uzbekistan, as a full truck or consolidated cargo.
- **The transit declaration.** Our own customs broker files it at customs.
- **CMR and TIR documents.** We prepare and check the international road transport documents.
- **Permits and the border.** We help foreign trucks obtain a permit (“dozvol”), meet the truck at the border and see it off.
- **Security for customs payments.** We help arrange the security (a guarantee) for transit cargo.
- **Afghan customs.** On the Afghan side, customs clearance is done by our partners — customs agents (declarants).
- **Tashkent.** We allocate space in a partner’s warehouse under customs control and reload cargo truck to truck or through the warehouse.
- **Cargo insurance.** For the duration of carriage.
- **Certificates and permits.** We help obtain a certificate of conformity, a phytosanitary certificate and other documents.
- **Sourcing and buying in China.** For Afghan businesspeople too: we find the goods, buy them and deliver them, and the manager states the terms before the order — see [Product & supplier sourcing](/en/services/sourcing/).

On this route we mainly carry three groups of cargo: consumer goods (clothing, household goods, electronics and similar trade goods), construction and finishing materials, and equipment and spare parts. If your cargo is something else, tell us what it is.

How to start:

1. **You write to us.** Tell us what the cargo is, its volume or weight, where it is now and where it has to go — Hairatan or Mazar-i-Sharif.
2. **We calculate the price and the time.** The manager calculates them for your cargo and proposes a full truck or consolidated cargo.
3. **Contract.** Payment goes through a bank that works between Uzbekistan and Afghanistan, under a contract between you and us.
4. **Documents.** We tell you which documents we need from you; we handle the transit documents inside Uzbekistan ourselves.
5. **Receiving the cargo.** In China, cargo for Afghanistan is received in Yiwu; from there it travels by truck through Kazakhstan and Uzbekistan. For cargo that is already in Uzbekistan, the manager tells you where we take it over.
6. **On the way.** You follow the cargo through our ERP system, in Telegram.
7. **Handover.** You collect the cargo at the border in Hairatan, or we deliver it to Mazar-i-Sharif.

Message us on WhatsApp (+998 90 175 78 00) or Telegram — that is where the work starts. Write to us in Persian or Dari — we answer in Persian with the help of a translator. We also answer in English, Russian and Uzbek. Details are on the page [Cargo to Afghanistan](/en/services/cargo-to-afghanistan/).

> **Manager’s tip.** The transit declaration is filed before or at the time the truck crosses the customs border into Uzbekistan (Customs Code, Article 261, part three; Cabinet of Ministers Resolution No. 737, Regulation point 6); filed before the crossing, it also makes separate advance information unnecessary (Resolution No. 737, point 2). So send us the invoice and the packing list before the truck sets off — that leaves time to check the documents.
